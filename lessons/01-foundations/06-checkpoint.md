## Ready for Module 2 if…

You can do each of these without looking back:

- Say what runs where: Ansible and its collections on the control node; SSH and Python on the managed nodes, with nothing left behind. (Lessons 1 and 5)
- Predict `ok` or `changed` for a rerun, and say why `shell` reports `changed` every time where a module does not. (Lessons 1 and 5)
- Write a YAML inventory with groups by role and by environment, group them with `children`, and check it with `ansible-inventory --graph`. (Lesson 2)
- Tell which `ansible.cfg` is in use, and which layer wins when a setting is set in several places. (Lesson 3)
- Target an intersection or an exclusion with a quoted pattern, and check it with `--list-hosts` before anything runs. (Lesson 4)
- Name modules by FQCN in playbooks, and look them up with `ansible-doc`. (Lesson 5)

If one feels shaky, reread the lesson in brackets before Module 2.
